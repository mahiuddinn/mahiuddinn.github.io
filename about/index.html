About Mahiuddin Mahi

I'm an independent SEO and digital marketing professional based in Bangladesh. I work with agencies, digital marketers, and website owners who need careful, manual link building support — and who want to know exactly what was done.

My background in digital marketing

My start was in general digital marketing — social media marketing, content management, and day-to-day website work on WordPress and Blogger. That is where I learned how content actually gets published, edited, and maintained, and why a link only has value when it sits inside an article people genuinely want to read.

Over time my work concentrated on the search side: keyword and competitor research with Ahrefs and SEMrush, monitoring performance in Google Search Console, and — most of all — finding and qualifying websites that are a real topical fit for a client's content. That research-heavy part of link building became the main thing I do.

What I focus on now

I concentrate on contextual link insertion, link prospecting, and blogger outreach. Three areas keep my attention:

  • Relevance — a link from a genuinely related page beats a bigger, unrelated site.
  • Editorial quality — real websites with real content and real visitors.
  • Documentation — every prospect, reply, and placement recorded in a sheet you own.

Working principles

  • No private blog networks, no link farms, no mass-comment links.
  • I don't promise positions in Google. I promise researched work and honest reporting.
  • If a placement isn't a good fit, I say so instead of selling it.
  • I only publish claims I can back up — including on this website.
  • You always know the current status of every prospect on your list.

How I communicate with clients

I work remotely with clients worldwide, mainly over email and Google Sheets, and I'm happy to join a call when a project needs one. My English is professional and clear; I aim to answer plainly rather than with marketing language, and I check anything ambiguous before acting on it. You can expect a reply within about one working day, a shared tracking sheet as the single source of truth, and regular updates rather than silence followed by a big reveal.

I'm comfortable writing briefs, outreach emails, and reports in English — and in Bangla for local clients.
