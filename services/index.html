Link Building Services, Scoped Clearly

Six services that cover the research-heavy, time-consuming part of link building — so your team spends its hours on strategy instead of digging through thousands of prospect URLs.

Honest note: no ethical SEO professional can guarantee Google rankings. What I can guarantee is relevant research, careful manual outreach, and full documentation of every link I work on.

Service 01

Contextual Link Insertion

A contextual link is placed inside an article that is already published and already ranking, in a paragraph where the link genuinely helps the reader. Because the page exists and has traffic, the link can pass relevance much sooner than a brand-new guest post.

What's included

  • Finding live, topically relevant pages that fit your target URL
  • Checking the page for existing outbound links and placement quality
  • Contacting the webmaster or editor and negotiating the placement
  • Agreeing anchor text and the exact paragraph position
  • Verifying the link is live, indexed, and dofollow as agreed
  • Delivering a report with URL, anchor, position, and date

Who needs it

  • SEO agencies filling a client's authority gap quickly and safely
  • Website owners with good content but few references to it
  • SaaS and affiliate teams supporting money pages or comparison articles
  • Freelance SEOs who want research and outreach delegated

How I work

Shortlist → manual quality review → personalised outreach → placement confirmed with a screenshot → tracked in your sheet. Sites that look like link sellers are dropped, not pitched.

Expected deliverables

  • Live contextual link(s) on relevant, existing pages
  • Spreadsheet: target URL, placement URL, anchor, DR/traffic, price, live date, screenshot
  • Contact details of each webmaster for future reference

Getting started

Send your target URL, the niche, an authority range you'd accept, and a budget per link. I come back with a shortlist and realistic expectations before any payment changes hands.

Service 02

Blogger Outreach

Manual email outreach to bloggers, editors, and site owners — for guest contributions, resource pages, partnerships, and placements. Every message is written for the specific site; nothing is sent as a blind BCC blast.

What's included

  • Personalised email sequences (initial + follow-ups)
  • Reply handling, negotiation, and relationship notes
  • Guest post pitch angles matched to each site's content gaps
  • Coordination of drafts, edits, and publication dates

Who needs it

  • Agencies running ongoing outreach without an in-house executor
  • Founders who want mentions in niche publications
  • Content teams building a pipeline of contributing sites

How I work

I read the site before writing to it. You approve the message template once, then I keep the thread updates coming in the shared sheet — replies, rejections, and positive signals all logged.

Expected deliverables

  • Outreach tracker: contact, site, stage, last contact date, outcome
  • Approved email copy and follow-up sequence
  • Confirmed placements or a clear "no" from each prospect

Getting started

Share your goal (mentions, guest posts, partnerships), your service or content, and the types of sites you'd like to appear on. I'll propose an outreach plan and a target list size.

Service 03

Link Prospecting

A vetted list of websites worth contacting — built from your niche, competitor backlinks, and content gaps, then filtered by real quality signals instead of vanity metrics.

What's included

  • Competitor backline analysis in Ahrefs or SEMrush
  • Topical relevance review of every candidate domain
  • Basic quality filter: traffic signs, ad load, outbound link behaviour
  • Contact discovery for each accepted prospect

Who needs it

  • SEOs who spend evenings building lists by hand
  • Agencies quoting link building without knowing the supply
  • Affiliate and niche-site teams expanding a link profile

How I work

Broad export first, then a manual review pass: I open the site, look at the content, and cut anything that wouldn't survive a client's inspection.

Expected deliverables

  • Prospect sheet: domain, URL, niche, metric snapshot, reason to pitch
  • Rejected list with the reason — so you can see the judgement applied

Getting started

Send two or three competitors and your target audience. I'll confirm the filters we'll use and the number of prospects per batch.

Service 04

Website Research & Qualification

Before you pay for a placement, someone should open the site properly. This is that check — done on a list you already have, or one I build.

What's included

  • Content quality, update frequency, and author presence
  • Spam signals: PBN footprints, excessive sponsored posts, link neighbourhood
  • Traffic plausibility versus claimed authority
  • Existing contextual links on the target article

Who needs it

  • Agencies vetting vendors' link lists before buying
  • Site owners checking links they already paid for
  • Freelancers who need a second pair of eyes

How I work

Each site gets a verdict — accept, maybe, or reject — with a written reason you can forward to a client.

Expected deliverables

  • Annotated research sheet with verdicts and reasoning
  • Short summary of the biggest risks found in the batch

Getting started

Send your spreadsheet of URLs. Tell me the niche and what you're trying to avoid, and I'll return the sheet with verdicts added.

Service 05

Link Opportunity Analysis

Which of your pages deserve links first, and which specific articles on other sites could host them. This connects your content to realistic placements instead of generic "high DR" hunting.

What's included

  • Review of your site's strongest pages by topic and intent
  • Matching each page to candidate host articles with contextual fit
  • Anchor text suggestions that read naturally
  • Prioritised roadmap: easiest wins first

Who needs it

  • Teams with content but no distribution plan
  • Agencies onboarding a new link building client
  • Owners restarting after a poor link profile

How I work

I map your URLs against real pages, so every recommendation names a specific placement possibility rather than an abstract category.

Expected deliverables

  • Opportunity sheet: your URL → candidate page → fit reason → suggested anchor
  • Prioritised action list grouped by difficulty

Getting started

Share your site and the pages you care about most. I'll return an opportunity map before you commit to any outreach volume.

Choosing an approach

Contextual insertion vs. new guest post

Typical differences I see when planning a placement.
FactorContextual link insertionNew guest post
Content effortMinimal — the article already existsYou write or commission a full article
Time to valuePage already receives visits and is indexedNew page needs indexation and traffic to build
Relevance controlChoose a paragraph that matches your page exactlyDepends on how the commissioned article ends up reading
Cost shapeUsually priced per placementPlacement plus content production time
Main riskWeak sites openly selling insertionsThin, low-effort posts that never get read
What I don't do: PBN links, comment and profile-link spam, paid links presented as editorial, fake engagement, or guaranteed positions in Google.
