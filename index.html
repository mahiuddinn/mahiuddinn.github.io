My Workflow

How a Project Runs

A predictable process from first brief to a documented, delivered link.

  1. Brief & Goals

    We agree on your target pages, niche, authority range, and what success looks like.

  2. Prospect Research

    I build and qualify a list of relevant websites using Ahrefs, SEMrush, and manual review.

  3. Outreach

    Personalised emails to webmasters and editors — no mass blasts, no spam.

  4. Placement & Reporting

    Links go live contextually, and I deliver a tracking sheet with every detail documented.
